Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

VelvetSearch is a mobile app and website operated by Evangelina Castorena, doing business as VelvetSearch, in California ("VelvetSearch," "we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version: We use your photos only to run your search, then delete them within about a minute. We don't sell your information. We don't show ads. Usage analytics are tied to your account ID, not your name or email.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider. We never see them.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to add one.
  • Photos you search with: see section 2.
  • Your activity in the app: searches and search history, saved items and boards, price alerts, brand preferences, reports you submit about listings, and whether you confirm or reject a product match.
  • Website forms: your email and answers when you join the waitlist, answer a survey, or contact us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: which features you use, products you view, and retailer links you tap. This is linked to your account ID, not your name or email.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, and a push notification token if you turn on notifications.

Information from others

  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Pro subscription, provided by Apple through our subscription provider. Apple handles all payments. We never receive your payment card details.

The app uses your camera only when you open it to take a photo, and your photo library only when you choose a photo from it.

2. How your photos are handled

When you search with a photo:

  • The photo is sent to Google's Gemini API, which identifies the item in it.
  • At the same time, the photo is uploaded to our storage and shared through a temporary link with our search providers, SerpAPI or SearchApi.io, which use Google Lens to find matching products.
  • The photo is deleted from our storage about one minute after your search.

We do not keep your search photos, sell them, or use them to train AI models, and our AI providers do not use them to train their models. Your search history keeps the name of the item found, not your photo.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app: visual and text search, price comparison, saved items, price alerts, and search history.
  • To manage your account and subscription.
  • To send emails and notifications you've asked for, such as price alerts and the weekly digest, plus account emails like verification and password resets.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • To review reported listings and keep spam and broken links out of results.
  • To prevent abuse and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. VelvetSearch has no ads.

5. Links to retailers and affiliate links

When you tap a link to a retailer, you leave VelvetSearch and go to that retailer's site or app, where their privacy policy applies. Some of these links may be affiliate links. The retailer or affiliate network may use cookies or similar technology to record that you came from VelvetSearch. When a purchase earns a commission, we receive information such as the commission amount, not your name or payment details.

Commission never affects your results. Here's how we make money.

6. Emails and notifications

Account emails, such as email verification and password resets, are part of having an account. Price alerts, the weekly digest, and push notifications are optional. You can turn off push notifications in your device settings and unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.

7. How long we keep it

  • Search photos: about one minute.
  • Account data: for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: you can delete your account in the app under Profile. Your profile photo is removed right away, and all other account data is permanently deleted 30 days after your request.
  • Usage analytics: linked only to your account ID. After your account is deleted, that ID no longer connects to you, and the data may be kept in that form.
  • Search result caches: results for a search phrase, not linked to you, are kept for 6 hours.
  • Backups: our database provider may keep backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

8. Your choices and rights

You can view and update your information in the app, delete your search history, and delete your account at any time. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Do Not Track: our website does not respond to Do Not Track browser signals. We don't track you across other websites.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and reputable providers to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

VelvetSearch is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Where your information is processed

VelvetSearch is offered in the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.
